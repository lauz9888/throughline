## Requirements

A "Create Goal" modal, wired to the existing but previously-inert "Goal" add-item menu item,
following the shipped Create Aspiration modal's structure closely but with a green color scheme in
place of purple: mandatory Title field, optional Description/Reason fields (each with its own
toggleable info-icon tooltip), and a "Milestones" section supporting an unlimited number of
dynamically added/removed milestone rows (each with its own mandatory-labeled title input and
per-row remove control), all living inside the goal modal rather than as a standalone concept. The
modal reuses the shared close/Escape/backdrop-click unsaved-changes confirmation pattern
(`openConfirmDialog()`), with a goal-specific dirty-check divergence: the presence of even a
blank milestone row counts as "dirty" and triggers the confirmation prompt. Saving persists a Goal
record (id, trimmed title/description/reason, an ordered array of non-blank milestone entries, and
a creation timestamp) to its own `localStorage` key, distinct from aspirations, with no in-app
listing view in scope (verified via `localStorage` inspection only, per the `add-aspiration` →
`aspiration-tiles` precedent of shipping a listing view as a later, separate feature). 48 numbered
requirements in total, including 15 WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility requirements (dialog semantics, focus
management on open/close and on every milestone row add/remove, unique per-row labels/remove-control
names, `aria-live` perceivability of dynamic row changes, contrast, and keyboard operability). Full
detail, including explicitly flagged assumptions (milestone fields limited to a title-only field,
per-row removal, blank-row exclusion at Save, no listing view), is in
`.workflow/add-goal/requirements.md`. No open questions remained; approved by the user on
2026-07-28.

## Solution

Rather than duplicating `aspiration-fields.ts`, its already-generic tooltip/field-building
mechanics were extracted into a new shared module, `src/field-kit.ts` (`createInfoIcon`,
`createTooltipText`, a generalized `createTooltipController()`, and a new generic
`buildTextField()`), with `aspiration-fields.ts` rewritten to call these helpers while its exported
surface and DOM output stay byte-for-byte unchanged (`aspiration-fields.test.ts` is the unmodified
regression proof). `src/goal-fields.ts` calls the same helpers with goal-specific copy. Milestones
get their own module, `src/milestone-rows.ts`, isolating the add/remove/focus/`aria-live` logic
(a never-reused, monotonically increasing per-row counter drives both each row's visible label and
its remove control's accessible name, chosen over renumbering-on-removal to avoid rewriting every
subsequent row's id/label/aria-label on every removal) so it's independently unit-testable without
a full modal fixture. `src/goal-modal.ts` (chrome/orchestration) and `src/goal-storage.ts`
(persistence, `GOALS_STORAGE_KEY = 'throughline:goals'`) mirror the aspiration modal's and
aspiration storage's shapes respectively. The green color scheme is applied via a new `.modal--goal`
modifier class (background `#e0f2e4`, Save button `#1e7a42`, hover `#155f34`, disabled `#a9c9b0`,
info-icon `#1e7a42`), computed to independently meet WCAG 1.4.3/1.4.11 AA contrast the same way as
the aspiration modal's purple; `openConfirmDialog()` gained one new optional
`dialogClassName` option so the goal modal's own discard-confirmation prompt also picks up the green
scheme, with zero behavior change for existing (purple) callers. `src/app.ts` gained one
`else if (label === 'Goal') openGoalModal();` branch; cross-modal mutual exclusion (only one of
Aspiration/Goal modal open at a time) needed no new code, falling out of the existing `root.inert`
mechanism already relied on by the shipped Aspiration/Edit-Aspiration pair. One new ADR,
`docs/adr/0004-dynamic-milestone-rows-pattern.md`, records the row-numbering and focus-management
design for this repo's first dynamically-growable/shrinkable repeating field group. Design review
ran 1 cycle with no changes requested. Full detail, including the requirement-coverage map,
file-by-file change list, contrast arithmetic, and risks/edge cases, is in
`.workflow/add-goal/design.md`.

## Accessibility

Per UI-facing accessibility requirement in `requirements.md` (Requirements 34–48), design decision
plus automated-scan coverage:

- **Req 34 (dialog semantics/accessible name)** — `role="dialog"`, `aria-modal="true"`,
  `aria-labelledby="goal-modal-heading"` resolving to "Create Goal"; covered by `goal-modal.test.ts`
  and the e2e/jest-axe scans.
- **Req 35 (focus to Title on open)** — `fields.titleInput.focus()` at the end of `open()`; covered
  by `goal-modal.test.ts` and the e2e focus-on-open assertion.
- **Req 36 (focus trap incl. dynamic rows)** — unmodified `createFocusTrap()`, which re-queries the
  live DOM on every `Tab`; covered by both the unit-layer Tab-cycle test (through two added rows)
  and the e2e Tab-cycle test.
- **Req 37 (focus returns to add-item button on close)** — `addItemButton.focus()` in
  `closeAndTeardown()`; covered across all close paths in `goal-modal.test.ts` and e2e.
- **Req 38 (programmatic labels, distinct per milestone row)** — real `<label for>` on every input;
  `Milestone {n}` per row from the never-reused counter; covered by `milestone-rows.test.ts` and
  `goal-modal.test.ts`.
- **Req 39 (Title required semantics; row inputs not required)** — `required`/`aria-required="true"`
  on Title only; covered by `goal-fields.test.ts`.
- **Req 40 (deterministic add/remove focus)** — add focuses the new row's input; remove focuses the
  replacing row / new last row / Add-milestone button in that order of preference; covered by
  `milestone-rows.test.ts`'s three remove-focus cases and the e2e keyboard-driven equivalents.
- **Req 41 (unique remove-control names)** — `aria-label="Remove milestone {n}"`; covered by
  `milestone-rows.test.ts`.
- **Req 42 (perceivable add/remove)** — focus move (Req 40) plus a `aria-live="polite"` status
  region; covered by `milestone-rows.test.ts`'s live-region-text assertions.
- **Req 43 (close control accessible name)** — `aria-label="Close"`; covered by `goal-modal.test.ts`.
- **Req 44 (keyboard operability)** — all new controls are native `<button>`/`<input>`/`<textarea>`;
  covered by the e2e Tab-cycle/keyboard-activation tests.
- **Req 45 (visible focus indicators)** — existing `:focus-visible` rule extended to
  `.modal__milestone-add`/`.modal__milestone-remove`; row inputs already match the existing
  `.modal__field input:focus-visible` rule; covered by the e2e visible-focus-ring assertions.
- **Req 46 (confirm-dialog focus/announcement)** — unmodified `openConfirmDialog()` behavior
  (`role="alertdialog"`, focus to cancel on open, focus back into the modal on cancel); covered by
  `goal-modal.test.ts` and the e2e discard-confirmation axe scan.
- **Req 47 (contrast)** — green scheme hex values computed via the WCAG relative-luminance formula
  (documented in `design.md`); independently exercised live by the e2e `@axe-core/playwright`
  `color-contrast` rule (not disabled at that layer).
- **Req 48 (Save disabled state exposed natively)** — `saveButton.disabled` (native attribute, not
  style-only); covered by `goal-modal.test.ts`.

**Issues carrying the `accessibility` label**: none. The single bug raised this run (#92) is a `ci`
formatting failure unrelated to accessibility.
